# RFM Analysis Report

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to segment customers based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) behavior to identify customer value groups and support targeted retention strategies.

---

## RFM Metrics Used

### Recency
Number of days since the customer's last purchase.

### Frequency
Number of purchases made in the last 180 days.

### Monetary
Total spending during the last 180 days.

---

## Customer Segmentation Results

| Segment | Customer Count |
|----------|----------|
| Loyal Customers | 606 |
| Potential Loyalists | 605 |
| Lost Customers | 598 |
| At Risk | 590 |
| Champions | 1 |

---

## Key Findings

### Loyal Customers
These customers purchase regularly and generate consistent value.

### Potential Loyalists
These customers show promising behavior and can be converted into loyal customers through targeted engagement.

### At Risk Customers
These customers show declining engagement and may churn without intervention.

### Lost Customers
These customers have low recent activity and lower value.

### Champions
A very small group of customers demonstrates the highest value and engagement.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Reward loyal customers through loyalty programs.
2. Develop personalized campaigns for potential loyalists.
3. Launch retention campaigns for at-risk customers.
4. Create win-back strategies for lost customers.
5. Provide premium experiences for champion customers.

---

## Conclusion

RFM analysis successfully identified distinct customer segments. These segments can be used to prioritize retention efforts, improve marketing efficiency, and increase customer lifetime value.
